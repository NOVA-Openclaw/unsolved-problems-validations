# S239 VALIDATE — rentosertib proteomic-age claim

Source: Zhavoronkov et al., *Nature Biotechnology*, 2026-09-07, DOI 10.1038/s41587-026-03286-y.
Trial NCT05938920 (GENESIS-IPF).

| Element of the claim under test | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Paper, venue, first author | CONFIRMED (Zhavoronkov is first and corresponding author; Gladyshev is senior author) |
| Trial basis | CONFIRMED with caveats: the aging analysis is EXPLORATORY/POST-HOC. It uses n=42 of the 71 enrolled (9-11 per arm), 12 weeks |
| "3-4 years" | OVERSTATED: -2.71 to -3.46 y vs placebo, at the WEEK-4 PEAK, best result in the 30mg BID arm |
| "6 independent clocks" | DISCREPANCY: 6 proteomic clocks, all run on the same Olink 3072 assay, mostly trained on UK Biobank -> highly correlated |
| "Proof-of-concept for dual-purpose trials" framing | CONFIRMED: the paper makes this claim itself |

## Synthesis
- The weakest link is the statistics, not whether the paper exists: one-sided Mann-Whitney tests at FDR Q<0.10, with 9-11 patients per arm; 9 of 18 comparisons significant in the best arm. That's hypothesis-generating, not evidence of rejuvenation.
- The peak comes at week 4, not at the end of dosing (week 12). Whether the effect lasts is still an open question; a peak partway through could be pharmacodynamic noise or a response to the disease itself (anti-fibrotic effect lowering inflammatory proteins), not a systemic aging effect. The paper can't separate "less IPF" from "slower aging".
- "Six clocks agree" is ONE measurement read six ways, not six confirmations. The effective number of independent readouts is well below 6.
- The track-level lesson still holds: the DESIGN idea (put aging clocks into disease trials) is sound and cheap. The EFFECT claim needs a pre-specified replication.
- Corrected wording: "Exploratory post-hoc analysis (n=42) of the Phase 2a GENESIS-IPF trial showed placebo-adjusted proteomic-age reductions of ~2.7-3.5 y peaking at week 4, across 6 correlated Olink/UKB-trained clocks; the paper itself proposes dual-purpose trials."

## Verdict
CONFIRMED (paper existence, trial basis, framing) with DISCREPANCY (magnitude overstated; "6 independent clocks" is not independent).
