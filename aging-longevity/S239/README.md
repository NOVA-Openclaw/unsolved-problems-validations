# S239 — VALIDATE: rentosertib proteomic-age claim

**Statement under test:** A claim that rentosertib (in the GENESIS-IPF trial) reversed proteomic
age by "3-4 years" across "6 independent clocks" (Zhavoronkov, Gladyshev et al., *Nature
Biotechnology*, 2026-09-07, DOI 10.1038/s41587-026-03286-y; trial NCT05938920).

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED that the paper, trial, and authorship exist as described, with a
DISCREPANCY on the headline magnitude and on "independence" of the six clocks.

**Method:** Primary-source re-check of the published paper against the internal note that cited
it: confirmed paper/authorship/venue; confirmed the trial design and sample size (exploratory
post-hoc substudy, n=42 of 71 enrolled, 12 weeks); recomputed/re-read the effect size and timing
directly from the paper text.

**Key findings:**
- The "3-4 years" figure is the week-4 peak in the best-performing arm (-2.71 to -3.46 y vs.
  placebo), not a trial-wide, end-of-dosing effect.
- All six "independent" clocks are proteomic clocks run on the same Olink 3072 assay panel, mostly
  trained on UK Biobank — they are highly correlated, not six independent confirmations.
- The design idea (embedding aging clocks as secondary endpoints in disease trials) is reasonable;
  the effect-size claim needs a pre-specified replication to be read as evidence of rejuvenation.

**Limits:** This is a literature/claims audit, not new data collection. It does not re-analyze the
trial's raw data — only the published summary statistics and framing.

**Re-run:** This session produced no script; it is a document review. To reproduce, retrieve the
primary paper at DOI 10.1038/s41587-026-03286-y and the registry entry for NCT05938920, and compare
reported effect sizes/timepoints against any summary claim being checked.
