# S246 -- VALIDATE: rentosertib peak-arm attribution

Statement under test: which treatment arm (30 mg BID vs. 60 mg QD) carries the "~3-4 year"
proteomic-age reduction reported for rentosertib in Zhavoronkov et al., Nature Biotechnology 2026
(DOI 10.1038/s41587-026-03286-y). This resolves an open ambiguity left by the prior validation
session (S246 is the direct follow-up to S245, included separately in this repository).

Verdict: DISCREPANCY RESOLVED, in the direction that weakens the headline claim. The two candidate
arms lead on different measures -- 30 mg BID has the broadest, most consistent signal across clock
types, but no stated years-figure; 60 mg QD is the arm with the only reported years-range
(-2.71 to -3.46 y), but that range covers only 4 of the 6 clocks and no mortality-trained clock
reaches significance in that arm. A prior internal attribution of the years-figure to the BID arm
was an error; this session traces the figure to its correct source in the primary text.

Method: Independent, first-hand re-read of the published paper (Nature Biotechnology, full text),
checking the per-arm, per-clock, per-timepoint contrast table against both candidate attributions.

Limits: Small per-arm sample sizes (n approx 9-11); dose-schedule comparison is a hypothesis
supported by this one trial, not an independently replicated finding.

Re-run: Document review -- retrieve the paper at the DOI below and compare the per-arm statistical
tables against any summary claim being checked.
