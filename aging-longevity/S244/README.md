# S244 -- VALIDATE: in vivo chemical reprogramming lipotoxicity claim

Statement under test: Mitchell and Gladyshev (Aging Cell, Jan 2026) identified toxic lipid droplet
accumulation and mitochondrial stress as a severe dose-limiting barrier for in vivo chemical
reprogramming, and the toxicity was specifically attributed to valproic acid (VPA).

Verdict: CONFIRMED (paper existence and primary dose-limiting-toxicity finding), with a DISCREPANCY
correcting a prior internal record on two points: a wrong DOI (which belonged to an unrelated
Drosophila study) and an incorrect claim that toxicity is VPA-exclusive -- a VPA-free cocktail (2C)
still produces the toxic lipid-droplet phenotype in vitro, though VPA remains a major driver of
systemic toxicity in vivo at the dose used.

Method: Primary-literature re-check (journal page, DOI resolution, bioRxiv preprint, laboratory
publications list) against the paper itself, cross-checking bibliographic details and the cocktail
compositions against the methods section.

Limits: This is a literature/claims audit. It does not reanalyze the underlying mouse experiments;
it verifies that the published paper says what an internal summary claimed it says, and corrects
where it does not.

Re-run: Document review -- retrieve the paper at the DOI below and compare cocktail compositions,
dosing, and reported phenotypes against any summary claim being checked.
